Basic principles Local first

Preplo is an offline disaster-preparedness learning app. Scenario cards, interactive simulations and the go-bag checklist are all built into the app; you need no account, no login and no internet connection to use the core features.

  • We do not collect or upload personal identity information such as name, email or phone number
  • Your region choice (Taiwan / Abroad) and go-bag checklist progress are stored only on your device and never uploaded to our servers
  • We do not use device sensors such as the camera, microphone or location

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version includes Google AdMob native ads (shown only in a separate slot within the safety-knowledge list and labeled "Ad"; simulations, the go-bag, settings and every step-by-step / first-aid screen never show ads). AdMob may collect the following to serve ads:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)

See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Purchases (Remove Ads)

The app offers a one-time, non-consumable "Remove Ads" purchase, processed through the official Apple App Store / Google Play payment systems. We never handle or store your card number or other payment details.

Emergency call buttons and rating requests

When you tap an emergency call button (Taiwan: 119 / 110; abroad: 112), the app only opens your phone's built-in dialer with the number filled in; whether to place the call is up to you. Preplo does not record, and cannot access, your call history or dialing activity.

After you have used the app for a while, it may invite you to rate it through a system feature provided by Apple / Google. The rating prompt is shown and handled by the system; we receive no account or personal data from it.

Content disclaimer

All scenario lessons, first-aid steps and myth explanations cite their sources (mainly official agencies) and are for general learning and preliminary reference only. They do not replace on-site judgment by firefighters, medical staff or rescue professionals. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number first and follow the instructions of professionals on site.

Children's privacy

Preplo is suitable for all ages. The app collects no personally identifiable information, regardless of the user's age.
