基本原則 本地優先

Growlo 是一款貓咪成長時光機＋行蹤地圖 App，不需要帳號、不需要登入。

  • 您拍攝的照片、房間格局、行蹤記錄、提醒設定，全部僅儲存在您的裝置本地儲存空間，不會上傳至任何伺服器
  • 不蒐集、不上傳任何個人資料
  • 不使用定位（GPS）功能，「行蹤地圖」是您自行繪製的抽象房間配置圖，不是真實地理位置

相機權限

「成長時光機」的對位拍照功能會請求您裝置的相機權限，僅在您主動點擊拍照按鈕時才會啟用鏡頭，不會在背景擷取畫面。拍攝完成的照片只會儲存在您的裝置本機，Growlo 不會讀取、上傳或存取您相簿中其他既有的照片。

通知權限

Growlo 會請求裝置的通知權限，用於在您設定的時間送出「該拍照囉」的定期提醒，這些提醒完全在裝置本機排程與發送，不會傳送任何資料出去。

匯出與分享

「分享」功能會在您的裝置上把照片重新排版成拍立得樣式的圖片（加上白框與日期），存成暫存檔後開啟系統原生的分享面板，由您自行選擇要傳給誰。整個製圖過程都在裝置本機完成，Growlo 不會上傳照片，也不會知道您分享給了誰。

免費版匯出的圖片右下角會有小小的 Growlo 字樣，付費解鎖後不再顯示。

成長冊（PDF）與播放

「成長冊」功能會在您的裝置上，把您選擇的照片排版成 PDF 檔（封面、拍立得內頁與封底），存成暫存檔後開啟系統原生的分享面板，由您自行選擇存檔、分享或列印。整個製作過程都在裝置本機完成，Growlo 不會上傳照片或 PDF，也不會知道您分享給了誰。

「播放」功能僅在您的裝置上，依拍攝時間順序連續播放您選擇的照片，不會傳送任何資料。

免費版的成長冊在照片超過 4 張時，只會匯出封面與第一頁；解鎖完整版後可匯出整本。

Core Principles Local-first

Growlo is a cat growth time machine and roaming map app. No account or login is required.

  • The photos you take, room layouts, roaming logs, and reminder settings are stored only in your device's local storage and are never uploaded to any server
  • We do not collect or upload any personal data
  • We do not use location (GPS) features; the “Roaming Map” is an abstract room layout you draw yourself, not a real geographic location

Camera Permission

The aligned-photo feature of the “Growth Time Machine” asks for your device's camera permission. The camera is activated only when you tap the shutter button and never captures images in the background. Photos you take are stored only on your device, and Growlo does not read, upload, or access any other existing photos in your photo library.

Notification Permission

Growlo asks for notification permission to send periodic “time to take a photo” reminders at the times you set. These reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device and no data is sent out.

Export and Sharing

The “Share” feature re-lays out a photo on your device as a polaroid-style image (with a white frame and date), saves it as a temporary file, and opens the system's native share sheet so you can choose who to send it to. The entire image-creation process is done locally on your device; Growlo does not upload the photo and does not know who you share it with.

Images exported with the free version carry a small “Growlo” mark in the bottom-right corner; it is no longer shown after the paid unlock.

Growth Book (PDF) and Playback

The “Growth Book” feature lays out the photos you select into a PDF on your device (cover, polaroid-style inner pages, and back cover), saves it as a temporary file, and opens the system's native share sheet so you can choose to save, share, or print it. The entire process is done locally on your device; Growlo does not upload your photos or the PDF and does not know who you share it with.

The “Playback” feature only plays the photos you select in sequence, in order of capture time, on your device; no data is transmitted.

With the free version, a growth book with more than 4 photos exports only the cover and the first page; unlock the full version to export the whole book.

No Ads / In-App Purchase

Growlo contains no ads at all—this is a memory-keeping app, and showing ads while you browse your cat's growth photos would ruin the experience.

A one-time “Unlock Full Version” purchase is available (full growth book export, no watermark on exported images, unlimited rooms; the free version has no limit on the number of photos you can take). The purchase process and payment information are handled entirely by the Apple App Store / Google Play, and Growlo never accesses or stores your credit card or payment details. Purchases are verified through the RevenueCat service; RevenueCat only receives an anonymous device ID and purchase records to confirm your purchase status. For details, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Photo Storage Reminder

Because photos are stored only on your device, they cannot be recovered if you delete the app, clear its data, or switch devices without backing up first. We recommend exporting and saving important photos regularly.
